HELPFUL INFORMATION

Student Resources

Quick starting points for common questions from prospective students.

What program should I choose?

Start with your interests, preferred subjects, strengths, and future goals. Use the program comparison tool, then ask the school about the official curriculum.

Where can I confirm admission requirements?

Contact the admissions office directly. Requirements may vary for new students, transferees, SHS applicants, and international applicants.

Can I ask about a program before applying?

Yes. Use the inquiry form below to prepare a question. This prototype does not send messages until a backend is connected.

Are tuition fees and scholarships listed here?

Fees, scholarships, and eligibility can change. Confirm current information and deadlines with the school before making a decision.

Announcements: Official announcements and scholarship updates are not connected in this prototype. Add verified school announcements here when an approved information source is available.
